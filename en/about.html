Our Story

About Imaien.

Bringing Japanese tea to every scene of life, enriching as many lives as we can.
That is the unwavering mission of Imaien.

Mission

Bringing Japanese tea
to every scene of life,
enriching as many
lives as we can.

Imaien is a tea shop in Gakugei-daigaku, Tokyo. Since our founding in 1920, we have been bringing tea to the people of our neighborhood for more than a hundred years.

What we cherish most is Sawakai: the time you take to brew a cup of tea and pause. A conversation shared with someone, or a quiet moment with yourself. Both are Sawakai. No two cups and no two moments are ever the same. That is why we treasure every cup as ichigo ichie, a once-in-a-lifetime encounter.

The tea shops of old neighborhoods were places where moments like these arose naturally. In the rush of our own days, we too have been carried again and again by the pause a single cup can give. Sawakai is open to everyone, on busy days and slow days alike. Through our tea and the places we create, Imaien brings a relaxed, free Sawakai, unbound by etiquette or expertise, into life today.

Behind every cup are the farmers who grow the leaves and the tea masters who finish them. Those who make it, those who deliver it, and those who savor it: we hope everyone connected to a cup of tea can be happy. Through Sawakai, Imaien will continue to be the finest companion to your every day.

Why We Sell Tea

Tea holds potential
no one has yet discovered.

Having spent so long in the world of tea, we came to realize that the true potential of Japanese tea has yet to reach most people. "Japanese tea is brewed in a teapot." "It's something you drink with wagashi." Beyond such fixed ideas lies a landscape of countless possibilities.

Japanese tea is not confined to the bowl. As an ingredient, as an aroma that shapes a space, as a therapy that settles the mind. As a base for tea cocktails, a material for confectionery, a companion in meditation. Its potential extends into territory no one has yet set foot in.

Imaien pursues that potential through a range of endeavors. To make Japanese tea freer, closer, and richer. Believing, from the heart, that tea enriches life — that is why Imaien continues to sell tea.
